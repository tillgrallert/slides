# establishing data culture(s)
## establishing data culture(s)

we strongly believe that we need to build a new and resilient *data culture* across all disciplinary (and social) domains. Such a *data culture* would help us to address the new episteme 

1. through a theoretically-grounded understanding of the fundamental epistemological and hermeneutical affordances of the post-digital moment; 
2. through developing the necessary quotidian practices, skill sets, and disciplinary protocols; 
3. and through negotiating new understandings of history as a discipline of societal and cultural relevance.
